G2fresh @VERSION@

An editor for the Clavia Nord Modular G2: it opens, edits and saves G2 patches and performances, connects to a G2 over USB (experimental), and plays patches by itself with the Emulated G2 (experimental).

This installer puts on your Mac, each one if you want it (you choose on the Installation Type page):

  • the stand-alone app, G2fresh.app, in Applications;
  • the VST3 plugin, in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3;
  • the Audio Unit, in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components.

Not included: Clavia's software. The Emulated G2 and the Classic look run on your own copy of Nord's free download, the Nord Modular G2 OS v1.62 Update for the Mac (nordkeyboards.com, in the Nord Modular G2 downloads). Keep the .dmg: G2fresh asks for it the first time you choose Synth > Connect to Emulated G2 or View > Classic Look. Everything else works without it.
